What We Offer

Complete Dental Care
Under One Roof

From routine checkups to transformative cosmetic procedures, we offer a full spectrum of dental services tailored to each patient's needs.

General Dentistry

Comprehensive oral health evaluations, routine exams, and all-round dental care to keep your smile healthy year-round.

Teeth Cleaning

Professional scaling and polishing to remove tartar buildup, prevent gum disease, and leave your teeth sparkling clean.

Dental Fillings

Tooth-colored composite fillings that restore decayed teeth seamlessly, blending naturally with your existing smile.

Root Canal Treatment

Gentle, effective endodontic therapy to save an infected tooth and eliminate pain — often completed in a single visit.

Tooth Extraction

Safe, pain-minimized extractions performed with precision — including wisdom teeth — to protect your overall oral health.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile-transforming aesthetic treatments including veneers, bonding, and smile design tailored to your facial features.

Teeth Whitening

Professional in-clinic whitening treatments for a noticeably brighter, more confident smile in a single session.

Family Dental Care

Comprehensive dental services for every stage of life — from a child's first visit to senior dental wellness.

Preventive Checkups

Routine oral health screenings and early-detection examinations to catch small issues before they become big problems.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Have a question? We've answered the most common ones below. Always feel free to call us for anything not covered here.

How do I book an appointment at Sami's Smile Studio?
You can book by filling in the appointment form on this page, or by calling us directly at +92 302 041 3613. We're open every day until 10 PM so there's always a convenient time for you.
What should I do if I have a dental emergency?
Call us immediately at +92 302 041 3613. We do our best to accommodate emergency cases on the same day. For after-hours emergencies, please call and leave a message — we'll reach back as quickly as possible.
Do you accept health insurance?
We work with a number of health insurance plans. Please contact us before your visit with your insurance details so we can confirm coverage and help you understand any out-of-pocket costs in advance.
How long does a teeth whitening session take?
Professional in-clinic teeth whitening typically takes 45–75 minutes. Results are visible immediately after the session. We'll also advise you on how to maintain your brighter smile at home following treatment.
Is this clinic suitable for children and seniors?
Absolutely. We are a family dental clinic welcoming patients of all ages — from young children having their first dental visit to older adults needing restorative or preventive care. Our team is trained to make every age group feel comfortable.
How often should I visit the dentist?
We recommend a dental checkup and professional cleaning at least every six months. Regular visits allow us to catch small issues early and keep your oral health in optimal condition year-round.
